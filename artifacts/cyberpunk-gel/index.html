GLYPHBOX / DARK PAPER EDITION

Dark retrowave/
cyberpunk cityscape

122 tiles retain the buildings, cars, boats and four-view rotations of the original city. Bright contours replace filled roofs and walls; windows become single light bars, and the grids leave more paper exposed.

ice whitehot pinkelectric cyanlilaclemon yellowmint

Reload glyphs, then select Use Dark retrowave/cyberpunk cityscape. This selects navy paper, outlines and six gel passes. You can switch to Black under Paper colour. Pen width starts at 0.45 mm; match it to your gel pens.

Screen colours illustrate the palette; actual ink appearance depends on your pens and paper.
