Medieval
Towns & castles

197 plot-ready tiles, using the Antiquity collection’s six inks. Timber framing, steep tiled roofs, cobbled lanes, walled gardens and a working medieval waterfront.

Each building and vessel is drawn from four directions. Compare the views across each row: the geometry turns on the ground, while walls and roofs remain upright.
